Where is Rose Homemade Foods?

What facilities does Rose Homemade Foods have?

South Indian meals, Diet plans, Customised meals, Budget meal plan, Weekly & monthly subscriptions, Non-veg two days a week, No artificial preservatives, Sunflower, groundnut & coconut oils only, Doorstep delivery.

How much does a monthly plan cost?

Breakfast, lunch and dinner from ₹6,000 to ₹7,500 a month. Breakfast only ₹3,500, lunch only ₹4,000, dinner only ₹3,500, any two meals ₹5,000.

Is non-veg available?

Yes, any two days a week: fish gravy, chicken gravy, biryani, egg, fish fry or dry fish gravy. Special non-veg orders are also available.

Which oils do you use?

Only refined sunflower, groundnut and coconut oils.

Jayaraj Dental Clinic

A family practice since 1972

Dr P. V. Balanarayanan (MDS) began practice in 1972 with two chairs in 300 sq ft on Nadu Street, Mylapore, and named the clinic after his grandparents Ethiraj and Jayammal. Today the clinic has five operatories on East Mada Street and a branch in Mandaveli.

Our doctors

A family of dentists

Dr Rajini Balanarayanan graduated from Madras Dental College in 1976. Dr P. B. Anand, MDS, is a periodontist trained in implantology, lasers and full mouth rehabilitation. Dr Praveena Anand holds a Master's in Orthodontics and Orofacial Orthopaedics.

Good to know

Where is Jayaraj Dental Clinic?

15/2, East Mada Street (opposite ICICI Bank), Mylapore, Chennai 600 004 (Weekdays 9.30 am–12.30 pm, 5.30–9.30 pm · Sundays 10 am–12 pm).

What facilities does Jayaraj Dental Clinic have?

Laser dentistry, Full mouth rehabilitation, Sedation dentistry, Teeth alignment & braces, Invisible & lingual braces, Preventive & child care, Implant surgery, Gum surgery, Root canal treatment, Tooth whitening, Painless extractions, X-ray unit.

What are your timings?

Mylapore: weekdays 9.30 am to 12.30 pm and 5.30 to 9.30 pm; Sundays 10 am to 12 pm. Mandaveli: mornings by appointment, evenings 5.30 to 8.30 pm.

Do you treat children?

Yes — preventive care, fluoride, sealants, tooth-coloured fillings and treatment for milk teeth.

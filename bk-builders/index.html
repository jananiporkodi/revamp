Good to know

Where is BK Builders?

BK Vidhya Rohini, No. 328/J, Viduthalai Nagar, 3rd Main Road, S.Kolathur, Kovilambakkam, Chennai 600117.

What facilities does BK Builders have?

Red bricks, river-sand plaster, Covered SUV-size parking, Automatic lift with glass shutters, EV charging bay, CCTV & access control, Potable ground water (2 borewells), Joint ventures for landowners, Construction on your plot.

What sizes are available at B.K. Aboorva Rohini?

2 BHK + 2 toilets (1,090 sq.ft), 3 BHK + 3 toilets (1,435 and 1,505 sq.ft), and top-floor 3 BHK (1,625 sq.ft) and 4 BHK (1,840 sq.ft) flats with a private terrace. Ask for the price.

Where does BK Builders build?

Kovilambakkam, S.Kolathur, Medavakkam, Pallikaranai, Madipakkam and Keelkattalai, a short drive from the S.Kolathur office.

Where is Sri Sai Catering?

New No. 3/1, Old No. 26, Sai Nivas, Amman Koil Street, Thathankuppam Main Road, Villivakkam, Chennai 600 049.

What facilities does Sri Sai Catering have?

Marriage & reception, Betrothal party, Seemantham & gruhapravesam, Birthday, Corporate events & seminars, Live snacks & chaat counter, Live fresh juice counter, Live fruit salad counter, Chocolate fountain, Welcome girls, Mehandi artist, Wedding hall booking, Vegetable & ice carving, Printed thambulam bags, Photo & video, Nadeswaram & melam, Florist, Theme decorations, Wedding car rental.

Do you cater both veg and non-veg?

Yes. Complete vegetarian catering with a vast menu, and non-vegetarian menus for marriages, birthdays and special events.

What else can you arrange?

Live counters, welcome girls, mehandi, wedding hall booking, photo and video, nadeswaram and melam, florist, theme decorations and wedding cars.
